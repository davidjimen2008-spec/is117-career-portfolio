# Web Development Career Research

**IS117-005 · Research checked November 12, 2026**

## Research question

How do a frontend engineering internship and a junior frontend engineering role differ in responsibilities, qualifications, and portfolio evidence?

## Follow one finding from source to decision

1. **Original evidence:** Open the [New York Times employer posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/thenewyorktimes/jobs/4751909005) and see the dated [employer role cards](./research/roles).
2. **Compare and interpret:** See how React, APIs and responsive design influence the proposed work in the [evidence-to-decision comparison](./research/comparison).
3. **Propose a feature and check:** Follow the requirement into the [portfolio feature and verification plan](./portfolio/plan). Tests are planned, not observed.
4. **Review the decision:** Use the [peer-review log](./review/log) to trace feedback, linked changes, verification and reviewer retry.
